Smart Alarm System

Automated daily bell system that triggers 3 times throughout the school day — with intelligent snooze logic to keep periods on schedule without disruption.

1ST
Warning BellRings once as a gentle heads-up. If not tapped, it stops automatically on its own.
2ND
Action BellRings again as a firm reminder. Staff or students must acknowledge to proceed.
3RD
Final BellRings continuously until the period fully ends, then stops completely on its own.
